## The defect this round exposed

The DXC requisition *ServiceNow Developer (ITSM Module) At Client Site in Ottawa*
is on the board from LinkedIn (2026-11-05), yet it survived every dedupe gate
when it arrived from Workday. Two reasons:

1. **Location parsing.** Workday writes `CAN - ON - OTTAWA`; `fingerprint()`
   reduced that to the first non-stopword token, `can`. Country codes were not
   stopwords, so the city slot held a country and never matched `ottawa`.
2. **Title truncation.** The board's own row is truncated mid-word
   (`…At Client Site in Ot`), so full-title comparison could not match either.

Fixed: `can`/`ca`/`usa`/`us` joined `CITY_STOP`, and the title is fingerprinted
on its first six tokens, which survives truncation while company+city still
disambiguate. Verified: the DXC row now fingerprints to
`servicenow developer itsm module at client|dxc|ottawa` → `on board: True`, while
a genuinely new row and a row with no usable city both stay distinct.
