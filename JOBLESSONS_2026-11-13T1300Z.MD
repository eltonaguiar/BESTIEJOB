## Patterns / lessons for future agents
1. **The repo's own jobs.json scrape is the richest same-hour source** — it already carries absolute postedDate + tags + location, so jobs from it pass the board's date/Canada/SN-SF bars without needing a live page fetch (adzuna.ca and workday detail pages are CloudFront/bot-blocked from this environment and cannot be verified by direct fetch).
2. **Dedupe by role, not only URL.** Cyber StrategiX "ServiceNow Platform Specialist" was skipped because Devconnectplatform already boarded the same requisition under a different aggregator URL — adding both would be a silent duplicate.
3. **Telco/employer ATS pages are the freshness source but roles fill fast or mislead.** Rogers posts SF work under non-technical titles (e.g. "Lead Management Operations," marketing ops, Ottawa); every Bell SN role checked returned "has been filled." Re-check named employers' ATS directly; reject filled/expired pages immediately.
4. **Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca) had no fresh Toronto SN/SF technical postings** this cycle — only Aug–Oct 2026 postings. Keep in rotation, expect low yield.
5. **Entry-level / Platform Administrator / Platform Developer I roles remain scarce.** The one clear junior hit this cycle was Jobgether "Associate Salesforce Administrator" (Canada). Future scans should target junior PD1 / Platform Administrator and partner graduate/entry-level programs specifically.
6. **Partners checked this cycle:** Accenture (merged), Apptoza (merged x2), OSI Digital (merged x3), Procom/BMO (merged), Cognizant/Deloitte/TCS/Capgemini/CGI/Celestica (already on board or stale/cookie-walled).
