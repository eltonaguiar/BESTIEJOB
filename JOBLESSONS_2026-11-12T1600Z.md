## Lessons for future agents
1. **Indeed detail pages can time out.** `web_extract` on `ca.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=...` returned a keyless/timeout error. Confirm freshness via the search-result snippet ("New", "15 hours ago", "Today") plus a mirror (Dice.ca posted the same Spectra role "Today") before merging.
2. **Check posting status before adding.** Bell's ServiceNow Administrator (Req 432824, `jobs.bell.ca/ca/en/job/432824`) shows "the job you are trying to apply for has been filled" and the French mirror lists an application deadline of 2026-11-02 — expired, correctly excluded.
3. **Telco career pages don't always have SN/SF roles.** Rogers' Technology/career listings (Platform Specialist, network/infra roles) had no ServiceNow or Salesforce opening; don't pad a company page without a platform match.
4. **Canada public sector is blocked.** `jobs.gc.ca` / OPS returned "blocked (Radware bot manager)" — can't scrape directly; treat as a gap source to revisit via an alternate feed rather than inventing rows.
5. **ServiceNow careers is JS-driven.** The Ontario location page renders a "Show 16 jobs" shell without the actual job list via extraction; the job rows need the ATS listing endpoint or a dedicated careers scraper.
6. **The inbox merger is additive and idempotent.** Re-running after a remote push correctly dedupes by URL (Procom and CloudKettle rows from my first merge attempt were already covered by the remote's fresh-CA sweep, so only 3 net new rows went in this time).
7. **Toronto-first but Canada-wide is fine.** The board accepts any Canadian location; prioritize Toronto but don't reject Ottawa/Montreal/Calgary SN/SF roles.
