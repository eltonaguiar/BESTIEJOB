## Workday CXS notes (what worked / what broke)
- CXS search endpoint: `https://<tenant>.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/<tenant>/<site>/jobs` with POST body `{"appliedFacets":{},"limit":20,"offset":0,"searchText":"<q>"}`.
- **limit must be ≤ 20**: `limit:50` → HTTP 400 (`{"errorCode":"HTTP_400",...}`); `limit:20` works.
- Required headers: only `User-Agent` (Chrome), `Accept: application/json`, `Content-Type: application/json`. Adding `Origin`/`Referer` triggered 400/422.
- `--compressed` required.
- urllib is blocked by Workday CXS; curl works.
- 103 postings seen across reachable tenants; 6 SN/SF-related matches, ALL non-Canada (Chicago IL, Quezon City ×2, Makati City ×2, Gurugram Haryana) — excluded under Canada scope.
