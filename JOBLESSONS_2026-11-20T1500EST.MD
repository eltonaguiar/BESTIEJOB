## Patterns / gotchas learned this run

1. **`fresh=505` was really 32 new URLs.** The scan's `fresh` counter includes Adzuna re-issues (new
   id, same role) and LinkedIn cards for roles posted 1–3 weeks ago that surfaced again in the `r86400`
   window. Diff the working `jobs.json` URL set against `git show origin/main:jobs.json` before
   reporting or hand-adding anything. Expect a ~10:1 fresh→new ratio at saturation.
2. **Workday 429 can persist well past `:01Z` — the bank boards stayed walled at `~20:08Z` (>6 min).**
   Unlike the 14:00 run (retry at :07Z got 200 on all 20 calls), this hour bmo/td/cibc/manulife/interac/
   accenture/iko were still 429 on a 10-tenant × 2-query retry; only caatpension, the Salesforce vendor
   board and gdit answered. **Mitigation that worked:** TD's 3 fresh roles and BMO's 2 fresh roles were
   already captured by **Adzuna (TD) and LinkedIn (BMO)** even with Workday down — the aggregators cover
   the bank gap. Do not treat Workday 429 as "no bank roles"; cross-check Adzuna/LinkedIn for the same
   banks.
3. **build_site mirror-collapse drops a new twin URL from the feed entirely — not into `alternates`.**
   A new LinkedIn URL for a company+title that already has a card under another URL (jobicy for
   Alimentiv "ServiceNow Platform Specialist", simplyhired for Altus "Salesforce Platform Developer")
   disappears from `jobs-data.json` (grep = 0). The role still renders under the surviving card, so
   **verify a new role by company+title in `jobs-data.json`, not by URL presence.**
4. **Applied rows are hidden by design.** Benevity "Senior Salesforce Administrator" is in `APPLIED.md`
   (2026-11-01, Ashby confirmation), so all 3 of its fresh URLs (Ashby + 2 LinkedIn) are "applied
   hidden" — correct, not a loss.
5. **OSI Digital posts SF roles in a 5-req cluster** (Lead, Developer Toronto, Developer Vancouver, QA
   Tester, BA). These are distinct reqs from one agency — keep all; dedupe on URL, never on
   company+title for agency rows.
6. **TD Bank is the most active Canadian bank end-user this week** for both platforms: Associate SWE
   (nCino/SF), Senior Solution Architect (SN), SWE III (SF Data 360) — a genuine entry-to-senior SF/SN
   spread in one Toronto employer. Watch TD Workday (td.wd3/TD_Bank_Careers) + Adzuna for TD.
