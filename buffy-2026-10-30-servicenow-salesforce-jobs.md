## The reusable search prompt

Paste this into any job-hunting agent (or into me) to reproduce this scan:

```text
Act as a job-search agent. Find me CURRENT (posted within the last 14 days) openings for
BOTH of these role families, and return them as a markdown table.

ROLE A - ServiceNow: developer, administrator, platform developer, technical consultant,
platform architect. The posting MUST mention at least one certification, and you must quote
the exact sentence that mentions it: CSA (Certified System Administrator), CAD (Certified
Application Developer), CIS-* (any), or another ServiceNow cert.

ROLE B - Salesforce: developer or administrator. The posting MUST mention either
"Salesforce Administrator" (ADM-201), "Salesforce Certified Administrator",
"Salesforce Platform Developer I" (PD1) or "Platform Developer II" (PD2). Quote the sentence.

For every row give exactly: Role title | Company | Location and remote/hybrid/onsite |
Salary if the employer published one (do not estimate) | Certification required or preferred,
plus the quoted evidence | DIRECT apply URL on the employer's own careers site if it exists,
otherwise the aggregator URL | Date posted.
Also give: a "Verification" column with CONFIRMED (I read the certification line) or
UNVERIFIED (matched a search only).

Hard rules:
1. Priority order of output: Toronto/Ontario first, then fully-remote, then remote-eligible
   Canada, then remote USA.
2. Skip duplicates - same company + same role posted on several boards counts once.
3. Skip stale postings (older than ~30 days or with a passed deadline) and say which you dropped.
4. Never invent a salary, a link, or a certification requirement. If you cannot verify,
   mark it UNVERIFIED rather than filling it in.
5. Exclude roles that merely *use* the tool (account executives, BDRs, sales, generic analysts)
   unless the job is building or administering the platform.

Deliver 20 ServiceNow and 20 Salesforce rows, then list every posting you rejected and why.
```

## Flags and judgment calls

**Confirmed certifications (2 of 40).** Only two rows have a certification requirement I actually read:
Panasonic (Platform Developer I required, PD II an asset) and Bell (CSA listed as preferred). Everything else is
marked **⚠️ Not verified**. This is the honest state of these feeds — the search results expose a company,
a title, a salary band and a skill list, but not the qualifications block. I did not upgrade those rows on inference.

**Salary is blank where the employer didn't publish one.** Workopolis omits salary on roughly two thirds of
these postings; I left those as "—" rather than modelling a range. Where a figure appears, it came from the
posting's own text.

**Dropped as duplicates:** PCI Panasonic Canada duplicated the Panasonic Salesforce Developer req (same
$50-58/hr, same Mississauga office). NTT DATA's Halifax Senior Production Support Engineer overlaps its Toronto twin.
Freelance-recruiter reposts of the same Deloitte and Accenture reqs were counted once.

**Dropped as off-target:** sales and pre-sales roles that surfaced only because they use the platform —
CloudKettle Account Executive, Duco SDR, Absolute Software BDR, Jostens Virtual Sales Associate, plus
marketing-ops and Gainsight analyst roles. Also dropped: Nesto and Nesto Cloud Salesforce QA Automation
Developers (QA, not platform build), and Docebo Salesforce Product Owner (no build or admin work).

**Dropped as stale or paused:** Doctors of BC Salesforce Administrator (Vancouver, remote) — reported paused.
Utility Warehouse-flavoured aggregator reposts of KPMG's Kitchener ServiceNow req were kept only once.

**US remote not delivered as a separate tier.** The SimplyHired US feed returned 172 Salesforce and 203
ServiceNow remote postings, but it rate-limited my requests (HTTP 403) before I could harvest and verify URLs.
Rather than list US roles without working apply links, I built the 40 rows from the Canadian feed that
resolved cleanly. If US remote matters, say so and I'll run that tier as its own pass.

**What is NOT verified here.** I did not open each posting and confirm it is still accepting applications.
Workopolis serves a cached feed. Two roles from an earlier pass today (Bell Req 432824, closing Nov 2;
Accenture R00356212, closing Oct 31) are in that earlier file, not this one — check those deadlines separately.

## Practical notes for a Toronto search

- **The Toronto ServiceNow market is employer-concentrated.** KPMG alone has four Toronto/KW developer reqs in
  this list at three different seniorities — if you apply to one, ask the recruiter which reqs are still open
  rather than filing four separate applications.
- **Consultancies are the volume channel:** Deloitte, KPMG, Huron, BDO, CGI, TEEMA, Pyramid, Recutify. They hire
  continuously and often staff the banks and telcos. The direct employers here are Lightspeed, Scotiabank,
  Brookfield, CAAT, Bell, Four Seasons, Moneris, MLSE, Equinix, Fidelity.
- **Rate vs salary:** agency/contract rows quote hourly (TEEMA, Pyramid, Brookfield, NTT). Convert before comparing
  — $62/hr at Pyramid is roughly $129k annualised before benefits, which beats several salaried rows on paper.
