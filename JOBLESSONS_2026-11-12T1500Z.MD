## Patterns / lessons for future agents
- **Dedupe by role + company + location, never URL alone.** The same requisition arrives under Adzuna/LinkedIn/Workday/employer-ATS URLs and language-variant careers URLs (Cognizant /uki-en, /ca-en, /emea-es). Adzuna also emits one URL per city for one requisition — dedupe by normalized URL first, then company+title+date; vendor twins are distinct rows and both stay.
- **Never invent a posted date.** Undated employer-ATS (Workday) rows and undated partner pages (Lane Four) are permanently skippable; the merger drops them rather than fabricating a date. Consider a separate "undated watchlist" instead of dropping.
- **Title rule is a hard filter.** A job *at* ServiceNow/Salesforce is not an SN/SF role (Senior CRM Account Executive excluded), and vendor sales titles are not technical roles. Rogers `Lead Management Operations` is Salesforce marketing-ops, excluded by title rule despite being Salesforce work.
- **Always check the canonical employer page before counting a role live.** Bell's ServiceNow Dev looked fresh on aggregators but the canonical page returned HTTP 410 "filled" — verified closed.
- **Incremental yield one hour after a full scan is low.** The 09:00 run already covered Adzuna (68 calls), Job Bank (11 pages), Eluta (15 pages), and gap scan (229 postings). The 10:00 run's value is in re-checking named employers (Rogers, Bell, GC, partners) rather than re-running broad feeds.
- **LinkedIn guest endpoint 429s after ~4 rapid queries.** Script paces at 1.8s/query with 2 workers — do not increase concurrency.
- **Adzuna detail pages can 500 via keyless extractors.** Trust the dated sweep file for title/company/location/date, then diff the URL set against the board before adding.
