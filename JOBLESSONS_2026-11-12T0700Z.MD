## Patterns / lessons
1. **Third consecutive null run.** Zero fresh table rows on most hourly runs is the norm — do not treat a null scan as a failure.
2. **Dedupe before trusting "fresh."** The same role arrives via employer ATS and LinkedIn under different URLs; normalize by role + company + location, not URL alone.
3. **Bell filled-roles gate holds.** All checked Bell ServiceNow reqs are filled; don't re-surface filled roles each hour.
4. **Indeed "New" tags + LinkedIn dates are not independently verifiable.** Capture the direct posting URL and date at search time, or skip. Never add without a verifiable direct posting URL + date.
5. **Partner career sites rarely publish dates.** Undated anchors (Lane Four, etc.) are logged but skipped by the merger — a dateable source beats an undated one every time.
