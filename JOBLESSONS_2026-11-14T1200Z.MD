## Patterns / lessons for future agents
1. **Row growth ≠ role growth.** Adzuna recycles detail URLs; the same role resurfaces under a new `adzuna.ca/details/<id>` with a recent `created` date. Dedupe by URL is insufficient — before adding, grep `jobs.json` for company+title and check `alternates`; if the role is already tracked, the new URL belongs in the existing row's alternates, not as a fresh row. `build_site.py` clusters mirrors into one card at render time.
2. **Posted-date stamps lag real time.** The 07:00 scan's newest *added* posting is dated 2026-11-13; nothing dated 2026-11-14 made it into jobs.json as a new role. Freshness oracle remains Adzuna `sort_by=date` + Workday `postedOn="Posted Today"`; `f_TPR=r3600` on LinkedIn is near-empty pre-dawn EST — use `r86400` and let the ATS sweep carry hourly freshness.
3. **Toronto Platform Admin/Dev I is thin.** Entry-level Salesforce Platform Administrator / Platform Developer I postings in Toronto are rare; most fresh hits are senior/architect/consultant roles or contract positions (OSI Digital, Cognizant, Deloitte, Altus Group).
4. **Mirror URLs accumulate fast.** 49 rows added this hour, 0 distinct roles — expect hourly row growth to mostly be mirror URL accretion, not new openings.
