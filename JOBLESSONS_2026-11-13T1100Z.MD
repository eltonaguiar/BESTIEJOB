## Patterns / lessons for future agents
1. **Cognizant posts fresh Toronto SN/SF roles directly on careers.cognizant.com with absolute "Date published" fields** — both the Nov 11 (SAM Developer) and Nov 12 (Salesforce Developer) postings were already harvested by prior runs this week. Prioritize it each cycle, but expect it to be picked up by the hourly Action scan too.
2. **Dedupe by role (title + company + location), not only by URL.** The TCS Salesforce Tech Lead surfaced via Alion (alion.io) but was already on the board via Adzuna (adzuna.ca/details/5961947653, posted 2026-11-11). Same requisition, different source URL — adding it again would be a silent duplicate.
3. **LinkedIn guest search is a login wall (HTTP 422).** Never add a LinkedIn-sourced role without a verifiable posting date; it will fail the merger's row-readability check.
4. **Telco/employer career sites beat aggregators for freshness, but titles mislead and roles fill fast.** Rogers posts its Salesforce work as "Lead Management Operations" (marketing operations, Ottawa — wrong function and wrong city); every Bell SN role checked returned "has been filled." Re-check named employers' ATS directly; reject filled/expired pages immediately.
5. **Government of Canada Job Bank had no fresh Toronto SN/SF technical postings this cycle** — only Aug–Oct 2026 postings (CSA/CAD ServiceNow roles already archived/stale). Keep jobbank.gc.ca in the rotation but expect low yield.
6. **Entry-level / Platform Administrator / Platform Developer I roles were scarce this cycle.** Fresh verified hits were senior/partner roles (TCS Tech Lead, Cognizant Architect/SAM Developer). Future scans should target junior PD1 / Platform Administrator specifically and re-check partner entry-level / graduate programs.
