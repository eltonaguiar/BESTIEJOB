## How this list is ordered

1. **Tier 1 — Toronto / GTA.** Location first, then technical depth (Developer / Engineer / Architect > Admin > Consultant > Analyst > pre-sales).
2. **Tier 2 — Fully remote (Canada).** Remote-first roles open to Canadian residents.
3. **Tier 3 — Remote-eligible Canada + rest of Canada.** Canada-wide remote plus other Canadian cities (Montréal, Ottawa, Calgary, Vancouver, Kitchener) that a Toronto-based candidate could take remotely or with relocation.
4. **Tier 4 — USA.** US-remote roles, listed for completeness and only where the title is genuinely technical.

Everything below was live at scan time. Postings churn fast — **re-verify before applying.** No salary is an estimate: ranges shown are as published in the posting.

## What I deliberately left out

- **Sales / pre-sales / customer-success roles at ServiceNow** — Client Director, Enterprise Sales Director, Solution Sales Executive, Principal Customer Success Executive, Area VP Solution Consulting. These are the bulk of ServiceNow's Toronto headcount, but they are quota roles, not platform work.
- **Non-ServiceNow roles that merely mention ServiceNow in the stack** (e.g. generic ITSM analyst postings) where the platform isn't the job.
- **The stale corpus in this repo.** `jobs.json` contains 59 ServiceNow-mentioning records but its scrape timestamps are **February 2026** — roughly eight months old. Several of those postings (Bell web developer II, Sun Life, EQ Bank, Interac) no longer appear in today's live scan. Treat that file as historical, not as an open-roles list.
- **Duplicates.** Bell's `ServiceNow System Administrator` and `Administrateur ServiceNow` are the same role in two languages; several Deloitte and Accenture rows are the same req posted per-city. I kept them separate where the location differs.

## Reading the market

- **Demand is concentrated in Toronto** — 62 of 161 Canadian openings. Deloitte alone accounts for six Toronto ServiceNow reqs (IRM/SecOps, CMDB, GRC, Alliance Ops, ITOM, DTC platform); Interac, Bell, Scotiabank, Brookfield, CGI, Cognizant, BDO, Huron and KPMG are all hiring the same skill set.
- **Salary bands for platform dev work are broadly $100k–$170k CAD** in the GTA, with Deloitte's Global Technology contractor-style bands clustering tightly at $85,000–$156,000 regardless of seniority in the title.
- **ServiceNow's own engineering org in Toronto is small** — the local headcount is mostly solution consulting and CS, with the genuinely technical Toronto reqs being Staff Database Engineer and Senior Machine Learning Engineer. The company's Canada remote engineering surface (cloud security, incident response, solution architecture) is larger than its Toronto office.
- **Remote is real but not dominant** — 38 of 161 Canadian ServiceNow postings are explicitly remote; most Toronto roles are hybrid, typically three days on site.

## Caveats

- Scan is a snapshot, not a subscription. Postings expire and repost constantly.
- No per-job deep link is included. Workopolis and SimplyHired build job URLs from session-scoped IDs, so a link copied here would rot within days; the stable search URLs at the top are the durable path. Company career pages are the second stop for any role you shortlist.
- Salary figures are the employer-published ranges and are frequently the *full* band, not the intended offer.
- Some Ontario rows come from staffing agencies (TEEMA, LTM, Maarut, emergiTEL, Astra North, StafinGo, Galent, Recutify, Forhyre, Tri-global). Agency postings often hide the end client until a screening call.
- I did not verify remote-eligibility language case by case. "Remote" on these feeds usually means the country of the posting; roles marked Toronto-remote generally require Canadian residency for tax reasons.
