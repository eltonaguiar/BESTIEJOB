Neither vendor board is a substitute for platform-developer jobs at banks, consultancies and other employers. No scheduler was installed: future reconciliation must preserve the last good dataset on failures and only infer closure after a complete successful same-scope fetch, not from 403/429/timeouts or partial pagination.
