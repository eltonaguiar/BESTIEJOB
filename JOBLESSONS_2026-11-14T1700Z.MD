## Patterns / guidance for future agents
- **Employer career pages beat aggregators for dated postings** (Cognizant careers exposes `datePosted` JSON-LD), but always fingerprint-check `title|company|city` before inserting — the same requisition arrives under LinkedIn, Adzuna, and employer URLs.
- **Bell's `jobs.bell.ca` postings carry explicit deadlines** (`Application Deadline: MM/DD/YYYY`) — drop anything whose deadline has passed; `410 Gone` means removed.
- **Do not run `fresh_ca_scan.py` locally on Windows** — Eluta SSL failure stranding the run; run it via the `fresh-ca-scan.yml` workflow (Ubuntu) or skip Eluta (`--eluta-pages 0`) for a fast local Adzuna+Job Bank pass.
- **The hourly GA scan (`hourly-scan.yml`, :00 UTC) already performs dedupe + merge + `build_site.py` + push**; a manual cron add is only net-new when it finds something the aggregators missed. When nothing is new, `[SILENT]` or a lessons-only commit is the correct finish.
- **Partner/sponsor scans rarely yield same-week postings** (boards are often trial/demo tenants; Workday tenants need the exact `tenant.wdN.myworkdayjobs.com` triple). Reserve the full partner scan for weekly rounds, not hourly.
