## Limits

- **CareerBeacon links are search links, not direct apply URLs.** CareerBeacon returns HTTP 403 to every detail-page
  request from this host, so the job IDs could not be resolved. Each `Search` link re-runs the exact company+title query;
  the posting is the first result. Treat those rows as strong leads needing one click to confirm.
- **Workday links are direct and stable** — they resolve to the bank's own ATS record.
- **Cert coverage is partial.** Feeds expose description previews, not full qualification blocks, so most rows read
  `<uncertain>`. Nothing was inferred.
- **"Open" = present in today's live source.** Not proof the ATS still accepts applications.
- Aggregator dates can reflect a repost rather than first publication. Randstad cards and CareerBeacon relative dates
  were converted to absolute dates against 2026-10-30.
