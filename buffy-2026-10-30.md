## Exclusions

- Postings older than 21 days — dropped, not listed as stale.
- Duplicate reqs across boards — collapsed on company + normalised title.
- Sales, BDR/SDR, account-executive, pre-sales and recruiting roles that only *use* the platform.
- Product-owner / scrum / customer-success roles that neither build nor administer.

## Limits

- **30 of 230 rows carry no published date** (Eluta omits dates on result cards). They are labelled
  `— (not published)` rather than back-dated. Everything else is 2026-10-10 or newer.
- **Salary published on 33 of 230 rows.** The rest publish none; nothing was estimated.
- **Cert coverage is partial.** Feeds expose description previews, not full qualification blocks. Rows reading
  `CSA`/`CAD`/`Platform Developer` had that text in the preview; the rest are `<uncertain>`.
- **"Open" = present in the source's live feed today.** That is not proof the employer's ATS still accepts
  applications. Confirm on the apply page before investing time.
- Aggregator dates can reflect a repost rather than first publication.
