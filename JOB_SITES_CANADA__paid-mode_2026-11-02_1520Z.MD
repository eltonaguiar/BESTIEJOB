**Caveat:** This document is compiled from knowledge, not from live verification of listings or current partner-tier status. Board existence, partner tiers, and corporate ownership drift over time. Treat the specific company names as targets to check, not confirmed fact. The structural guidance — which channel type serves which purpose — is the durable part.

## 1. Where the volume actually is

| Site | Why it matters for these roles |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn Jobs | Highest hit rate for "ServiceNow Developer" / "Salesforce Developer" in Toronto. Recruiters search here first. Set alerts with exact product names — generic "ITSM Developer" misses roughly half the pool. |
| Indeed Canada (indeed.ca) | Largest raw volume. Best for contract postings from staffing agencies. |
| Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca) | Federal board. Non-negotiable for public-sector and Crown corporation roles (SSC, CRA, DND, Ontario Health). Many ServiceNow/Salesforce government contracts post only here due to procurement rules. |
| Glassdoor Canada | Overlaps LinkedIn but surfaces salary bands — useful for anchoring contract rate negotiations. |
| Talent.com (formerly Neuvoo) | Aggregator; catches postings from smaller regional boards that LinkedIn misses. |

## 2. Tech-specialist and secondary boards

- **Dice** — thin Canadian presence but non-zero for ServiceNow/Salesforce contract work.
- **Eluta.ca** — Canadian aggregator that indexes many employer career pages directly. Good backfill.
- **ZipRecruiter Canada** — moderate volume, reasonable contract representation.
- **Hired / Otta / Wellfound** — product-company skewed; low yield for enterprise-platform work. Low priority.
- **Stack Overflow Jobs** — shut down. Do not bother.
- **Workopolis** — believed to have ceased operating; verify before spending time.

## 3. Staffing firms — where most Canadian SN/SF contracts actually flow

In the Canadian enterprise market a large share of ServiceNow and Salesforce contract work is never posted publicly; it routes straight to preferred-vendor staffing firms. Registering with these is higher-yield than browsing boards.

- **S.i. Systems** — dominant in Canadian IT contract; very large SN/SF requisition flow.
- **Procom** — Toronto-heavy, strong bank and telco pipelines.
- **TEKsystems** — places SN/SF contractors into the big-5 banks.
- **Apex Systems** — same profile as TEKsystems.
- **Robert Half Technology** — permanent and contract, broad coverage.
- **Randstad / Experis, Hays, Robert Walters, Altis Recruitment, Eagle Professional Resources, iPartner Staffing, Akkodis (formerly Modis), Raise, CorGTA, Insight Global.**

Practical note: a recruiter at two or three of these will surface roles weeks before they appear publicly. Given the CIBC / BMO / Metrolinx history, Procom, TEKsystems, and S.i. Systems are the natural first three calls — they already hold the bank requisitions.

Why this matters for this candidate: partner firms bill certifications directly to clients, so a dense CIS cert portfolio is an asset there rather than an oddity. This is the highest-fit channel for someone holding 12+ CIS certs.

## 5. End-user enterprises worth monitoring directly

These organizations run large SN or SF estates and post on their own career portals, often ahead of aggregators.

- **Banks / insurance** — RBC, TD, BMO, CIBC, Scotiabank, National Bank, Desjardins, Manulife, Sun Life, Canada Life. CIBC and BMO already appear on the resume; internal referrals are the fastest path.
- **Telecom** — Bell, Rogers, Telus.
- **Government / public sector** — Shared Services Canada, Canada Revenue Agency, DND, Ontario Health, Metrolinx, TTC, Hydro One, City of Toronto, provincial health authorities.
- **Transport / logistics** — Air Canada, CN, CPKC.
- **Retail / grocery** — Loblaw, Metro, Sobeys, Couche-Tard.
- **HCM / ERP adjacency** — Dayforce (Ceridian), Workday Canada, SAP SuccessFactors shops. These are not SN/SF roles per se, but they hire profiles with integration and data-reconciliation experience — the natural pivot if the platform-specific market tightens.

## 6. Community and events — the highest-leverage channel

This is where "relationships with ServiceNow or Salesforce" converts into offers.

### ServiceNow

- **ServiceNow Toronto User Group (SNUG)** — free, local, attended by the hiring managers for bank and telco ServiceNow programs.
- **ServiceNow World Forum Toronto** — the Canadian flagship event.
- **ServiceNow Knowledge** (annual, Las Vegas) and **CreatorCon** — where partner firms recruit.
- **ServiceNow Rising Star / MVP programs** — visibility route into the ecosystem.

### Salesforce

- **Salesforce World Tour Toronto** — Canadian Dreamforce spinoff; heavy partner and end-user attendance.
- **Trailblazer Community — Toronto Salesforce Developer Group** — developer-focused, low-formality, high-referral-value.
- **Salesforce Saturday Toronto** — community-run, free.
- **Dreamforce and TrailblazerDX** — expensive; attend via a sponsor's booth list if possible.

Strategic note: given the two-platform profile, the SNUG + Trailblazer combination is unusually valuable. Very few candidates show up credible in both ecosystems, and the people who run those groups are the ones who forward resumes internally.
