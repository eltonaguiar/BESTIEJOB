## Lessons for future agents

1. **LinkedIn guest API now serves a wall on repeated calls — detect it by size/hash, not status.** Four consecutive `seeMoreJobPostings` fetches all returned HTTP 200 with **len 337822 and identical md5** — that is the authwall/challenge page, not cards (healthy card HTML is ~31 KB and differs per query). Check `status` **and** a length/digest distinctness test; on a wall, fall back to `new_tab` + reading `li a[href*="/jobs/view/"]` from the search DOM (118 cards, no auth, works reliably at ~6 s per query).
2. **`f_TPR=r3600` is nearly empty in the pre-dawn EST hours; use `r86400`.** The last-hour window returned 1 role for Toronto ServiceNow at 04:00 local. Freshness per hour belongs to the ATS sweep; LinkedIn's value here is the 24 h window.
3. **A repost with a renamed title is still a mirror.** lululemon's req moved "Salesforce Service Cloud" → "Agentforce/Salesforce"; company+title fuzzy match missed it because the board rows carry `company="Unknown"` and the company name inside the *title*. When a board row's company is Unknown, match on title substrings + location, not company.
4. **Workday tenant discovery: only the already-known pairs answer.** `bmo/External`, `td/TD_Bank_Careers`, `cibc/search`, `manulife/MFCJH_Jobs`, `interac/interac`, `caatpension/Careers`, `iko/IKO_Careers`, `gdit/External_Career_Site`, `salesforce.wd12/External_Career_Site`, `accenture.wd103/AccentureCareers` = 200. Guessed sites (`capgemini/Capgemini_Careers`, `kyndryl`, `ibm`, `cgi`, `cognizant`, `telus`, `rogers`, `kpmg`, `scotiabank`, `intact`, `loblaw`, `cprail`) all **422**; `rbc`, `pwc`, `sunlife` 404. Do not burn calls guessing — go through the employer's careers page or a search engine.
5. **Rogers exposes a clean date-sorted table; Bell exposes nothing.** `jobs.rogers.com/search/?q=&sortColumn=referencedate&sortDirection=desc` gives `<tr>` rows with absolute dates (`Nov 12, 2026`) — parse that instead of keyword search. `jobs.bell.ca` search renders an empty shell to a raw fetch and "(0)" in a browser: treat Bell as **zero SN/SF reqs**, not as a scraping failure, and re-check weekly.
6. **Ontario/gojobs and jobs.gc.ca are both behind Radware.** No amount of retrying helps; use Job Bank as the public-sector proxy but only trust postings whose *title* names the platform (this hour: 19 results, 0 title matches, newest 8 days old).
7. **Vendor rows worth keeping are those naming the platform in the title.** Avanciers posts ~6 Salesforce/ServiceNow reqs a week; KTek posts SN architects. Both are legitimate apply targets (separate competition each), so dedupe on URL, never on company.
