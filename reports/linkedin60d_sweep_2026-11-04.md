# 60-day sweep of the short-window sources — 2026-11-04

## What was asked

The hourly passes give the ATS/board inventory a 60/90-day window (`fresh_scan.py`), but three
sources only ever run on their own short defaults: `employer_sitemap_scan.py` (30 days),
`ats_fingerprint_scan.py` (45 days) and `linkedin_guest_scan.py` (3 days). This sweep re-ran all
three at **60 days** to see whether anything outside the current board turns up.

The two employer passes are saturated: every hit inside 60 days is already on the board, so their
short windows are not losing anything.

## LinkedIn batch — how the 127 rows were narrowed to 101

Every candidate URL was fetched individually (`ca.linkedin.com/jobs/view/...`) and the date was read
from the live page's `datePosted`, not from the card:

- 126/127 returned HTTP 200; the one 429 was retried after a cooldown and recovered.
- **115** returned a live `datePosted` and were kept.
- **11** returned 200 but no date — spot checks showed these carry *"No longer accepting
  applications"*, i.e. closed postings. They were dropped rather than published with a card date.
- 2 kept rows were reposted: the live date (2026-11-04) is newer than the card date
  (2026-10-14 / 2026-10-13), so the live date is what went in.
- Merger then dropped 13 duplicates and 1 marketing-function row, leaving **101 new rows**.

Dates therefore all come from the posting page; nothing was inferred from "3 days ago" text.
