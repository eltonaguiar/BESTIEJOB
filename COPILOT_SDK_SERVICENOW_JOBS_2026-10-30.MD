## Search notes

- The Ontario careers page directly surfaced current Toronto postings, and the individual official job pages above were checked for availability and role details.
- The careers site's filtered result pages returned the same unfiltered result set in this scan. Therefore, this report does not rely on those filters for remote eligibility or claim a complete count of openings.
- Some official pages now return 404 (including older Toronto postings surfaced in search results); those were omitted rather than presented as open.
- “Remote,” “flexible,” and a location label are not interchangeable. Confirm the role's assigned work persona, eligible hiring geography, clearance requirements, compensation currency, and current status on the application page.
